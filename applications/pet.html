CASE 01 / MATERIAL ENGINEERING

Automatic-feeder housing

Material in the corporate caseABS
Application challenge
The housing needs easy cleaning and consistent appearance, with food-contact and non-contact areas identified.
Material approach
The corporate case describes ABS for food-contact requirements; verify the grade, color and contact conditions individually.
Validation focus
Confirm migration documentation for contact time, temperature and target market; also assess cleaning, odor and snap-fit life.

CASE 02 / MATERIAL ENGINEERING

Pet travel crate

Material in the corporate casePP + impact modifier
Application challenge
Handling drops, load and cold environments require structural toughness.
Material approach
The corporate case uses impact-modified PP with weathering requirements; assess the material with ribs and latch design.
Validation focus
Test whole-crate drops, latches and loading at the target low temperature; check toughness and appearance after aging.
