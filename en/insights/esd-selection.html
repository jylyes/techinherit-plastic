Antistatic materials: why define resistance targets first?

Static control requires clear targets and consistent test and operating conditions.

On this pageDefine the purpose of static controlConfirm test conditionsValidate structural behavior too
01

Define the purpose of static control

Electronic-component protection, dust control and equipment interference may call for different priorities. Define the application objective before discussing antistatic or conductive materials.

02

Confirm test conditions

Surface resistance and volume resistance are different measures. Share the target range, test method, temperature, humidity and operating environment to make material proposals comparable.

03

Validate structural behavior too

Evaluate static-control compounds alongside impact, stiffness, surface and processing needs. Confirm the final choice through grade documentation and application validation.

This article provides general material-selection guidance, not a technical data sheet. Confirm the grade, operating conditions and validation requirements for your project.
