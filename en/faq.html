ASK THE RIGHT QUESTIONS

Find clarity for your next step.

01What information is needed for material selection?+

Please provide service temperature, loading, contact media, dimensional and appearance requirements, applicable regulations and expected quantities. Drawings, current materials and failed samples can also help.

02Can material compounds be customized?+

Techinherit’s corporate website describes materials R&D and custom-compounding capabilities. Discuss reinforcement, toughening, thermal stability, weathering or static control; specific proposals and development plans require engineering assessment.

03How should PA6 and PA66 be compared?+

Start with operating conditions and structural needs, then compare specific grades. Consider reinforcement, compound formulation, moisture condition and test methods rather than deciding solely by base-resin name.

04Can I request a TDS, SDS or batch documents?+

Tell our materials team the specific grade and documents needed. Confirm the content and scope of technical data sheets, safety data sheets and batch inspection documents separately.

05Why are universal performance values not listed?+

Properties vary with the grade, compound system, sample condition and test method. These pages describe material directions; use confirmed official documents and validation results for design and purchasing.

06How should antistatic requirements be specified?+

Define surface or volume resistance targets, test methods, environment and the purpose of static control, along with mechanical, appearance and processing requirements.

07What should be considered for food-contact applications?+

Specify the target market, food type, contact temperature and duration, cleaning and repeated-use conditions. These requirements guide material selection and validation.

08Can we validate samples first?+

Discuss performance targets, required quantities and a validation plan first. Engineering and commercial teams confirm sample, development and delivery arrangements for each project.

09How can I request pricing and lead times?+

Provide the material direction, expected quantity, color or custom requirements, destination and timing so the team can assess pricing and scheduling.
