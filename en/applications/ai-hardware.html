CASE 01 / MATERIAL ENGINEERING

Server fan cover

Material in the corporate casePBT GF30 FR
Application challenge
A lightweight thin-wall structure needs stiffness, dimensional stability and operating reliability.
Material approach
The corporate case uses flame-retardant reinforced PBT, considering flow, reinforcement and precision molding together.
Validation focus
Verify thin-wall filling, warpage, mounting-point fatigue and clearances after heating; assess noise in the assembled system.

CASE 02 / MATERIAL ENGINEERING

Edge-computing enclosure

Material in the corporate casePC GF20 FR
Application challenge
Structural protection, temperature rise and electromagnetic requirements need separate specifications.
Material approach
The corporate case uses flame-retardant glass-fiber reinforced PC; structural reinforcement and EMC targets require separate validation.
Validation focus
Verify thermal cycling, mounts, flame behavior at actual thickness and system EMC; glass-fiber reinforcement alone does not establish shielding.
