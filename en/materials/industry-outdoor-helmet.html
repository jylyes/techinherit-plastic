APPLICATION / REQUIREMENTS

Define the material through the part requirements.

01

Part function & scope

Distinguish accessories, mounts and protective components; define intended use and validation scope.

02

Outdoor environment

Record minimum and maximum service temperatures, sunlight, sweat, cleaners and storage conditions.

03

Buckles & repeated use

Review adjustment mechanisms, fasteners and repeated mating, focusing on weak sections and wear.

PART / PROCESS / VALIDATION

Confirm performance with the actual part.

Confirm the grade, part drawing and equipment before drying, molding and part trials; use formal documentation and trial results for processing parameters.

01

Environment: assess appearance and structure after the target sunlight, temperature, sweat and cleaning exposure.

02

Use: check adjustment cycles, buckle mating, mounting strength and aged performance.

03

Impact: assess the finished part against its intended use and applicable specification; catalog information does not replace protective-product validation.
