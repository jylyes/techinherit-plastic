SELECTION / APPLICATION

Define the material through the part requirements.

Define the matrix, carbon-fiber content and fiber length before evaluating structure and processing.

Typical components for discussion
Lightweight supports, precision frames and high-stiffness structures
Processing considerations
Review retained fiber length, orientation, equipment wear and appearance; distinguish matrix and fiber formulations.

BEFORE SELECTION

Before selecting, confirm

  • Temperature, loads and service life
  • Media, humidity and exposure
  • Geometry, wall thickness and processing
  • Appearance, color and applicable requirements
  • Sample condition and test methods
